# The World Today — Thursday, 26 November 2026

A newspaper front page summing up the world news of Thursday, 26 November 2026, built for the [make.fun news-image-20261126 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126).

The hero story is the Cairo ring-road inferno: a fuel tanker overturned and exploded on the city's ring road, killing at least 30 people, injuring more than 15, and leaving 27 vehicles charred. Four smaller panels cover the US unveiling sweeping new tariffs on imports from China, the EU, Brazil and others; Colombian police storming a Cartagena supermarket and freeing every hostage after a 17-hour siege; Huawei being found guilty in a trade-secrets theft case; and RSF drone strikes killing at least 19 civilians in Damazin, Sudan. A footer strip closes the page on a brighter note — China sending Pakistan's first astronaut to its Tiangong space station — alongside the news that the COP31 climate talks failed to reach a deal.

## How it works

Running `main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` byte for byte.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
